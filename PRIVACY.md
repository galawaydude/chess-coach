# Privacy Policy: Chess Coach

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Chess Coach is an open-source app (GPL-3.0, source at https://github.com/galawaydude/chess-coach). This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What the app does with your data

- **Your chess.com username.** When you tap *Load games*, the app sends the username you typed to chess.com's public API (`api.chess.com`) to download that player's public games. This request goes straight from your device to chess.com. It does not pass through any server run by the developer. chess.com's own privacy policy applies to that request.
- **Games, analysis, puzzles and settings** are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. They never leave your device.
- **Chess analysis** runs on your device with the Stockfish engine. No positions or games are uploaded anywhere.
- **"Copy prompt for Claude"** only copies text to your clipboard. The app sends nothing; you decide where to paste it.

## What the app does not do

- No accounts, sign-in or passwords.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
- No data is collected by, sold to, or shared with the developer or anyone else.

## Deleting your data

Clear the app's storage (Android Settings → Apps → Chess Coach → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app. This deletes everything the app stored.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
