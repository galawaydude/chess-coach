## Full description (max 4000)
Chess Coach turns the games you actually played into lessons.

Enter your chess.com username and the app downloads your public games. Stockfish then analyses every move right on your phone. There's no account, no subscription, and nothing is uploaded.

GAME REVIEW
• Accuracy for you and your opponent
• Every move rated: Brilliant, Great, Best, Excellent, Good, Book, Inaccuracy, Mistake, Miss, Blunder
• Evaluation graph, evaluation bar and coloured arrows for the best moves
• The name of the opening you played

A COACH, NOT JUST AN ENGINE
• Plain-English explanations: "this left your knight on f6 unprotected", "this walks into a fork", "you had checkmate in 2"
• Tap any move in an explanation to see it on a mini board
• A walkthrough of the whole game: what your opponent was up to, what you played, and what you could have played
• A game report split into opening, middlegame and endgame, with a tip for each

PUZZLES FROM YOUR OWN MISTAKES
• Every mistake and blunder becomes a puzzle from your own game
• Wrong answer? It explains why and shows how your opponent punishes it
• Then play the position out against Stockfish, branch into new ideas, or switch sides and play as your opponent
• Spaced repetition brings missed puzzles back until they stick

YOUR PATTERNS
• Accuracy and rating trends
• Which phase of the game costs you the most points
• Your most common mistakes, with tips
• Win rates for your openings

COPY A LESSON PROMPT
One tap copies a detailed coaching prompt (your game, your mistakes, the engine's verdicts) that you can paste into an AI assistant for a longer lesson.

Free and open source (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/galawaydude/chess-coach
